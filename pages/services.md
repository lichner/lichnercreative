Secure, cost-controlled cloud foundations, designed before the first
workload lands.

migrations, architecture and security reviews, and cost optimisation for

your team understands the platform they own.

Led by architects who have designed cloud platforms for enterprises

business needs.

Custom software, built by senior engineers and delivered working.

## DevOps and platform engineering {#platform}

Ship faster, break less, sleep better.

We build the engineering foundations under your software: delivery

the monitoring to run production with confidence.

For organisations at scale we design enterprise AI and machine-learning

## Automation and AI {#ai}

Practical AI adoption, from first roadmap to production.

A fixed-price discovery engagement maps your processes with the people
who run them, scores every opportunity by value against effort, and hands
you a twelve-month plan with costs and priorities. A pilot follows: one
process, one measurable target, four to six weeks, built on the Microsoft
platform you already pay for. Approval stays with humans wherever a
customer or a dollar is involved.

When your automation needs an agent, you get a team with production
agent systems and guardrails behind them, not a webinar certificate.

After handover your team runs the automation. We stay available as an
advisor, not a dependency.
